## 1. Executive Problem Framing & Market Opportunity

Enterprises across E-Commerce, Retail, Logistics, Healthcare, and Financial Services invest upwards of **$18M annually** on modern data stacks (Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Power BI, Looker). Yet, during critical operational incidents, executive leadership remains in the dark.

### The Two Fundamental Flaws of Modern BI
1. **The Context Blindness Crisis**: Traditional BI only reads structured rows in SQL tables. When revenue in a key region drops by **19.2%**, the dashboard cannot discern whether the drop is due to courier shortages in the warehouse, a competitor's aggressive flash discount, or a silent tracking tag failure on the checkout page. Unstructured data (Zendesk customer complaints, competitor web crawls, fleet telematics) remains disconnected.
2. **The Operational Latency Penalty**: Triangulating root causes today requires manual assembly of cross-functional "tiger teams" (Data Analysts, Operations Leads, Marketing Managers). This diagnostic cycle takes **4 to 7 business days**, during which:
   - Thousands of dollars in margin evaporate per hour.
   - Disgruntled customers churn permanently to competitors.
   - Operators execute blind, uncoordinated reactive discounting that further erodes profitability.
